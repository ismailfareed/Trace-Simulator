Telecom Trace Simulator

Upload a tcpdump capture (.pcap or .pcapng). The tool detects the protocol of each message automatically (SMPP, Diameter for Gy/Gx/Rx charging and policy, GTPv2-C for SGW–PGW S5/S8, PFCP for SMF–UPF N4) and prints every header field and parameter. Everything runs in your browser; the file is never uploaded.
